An agent MUST NOT ratify, retire, or alter this standard's normative

This record only presents the survey outcome and the operator's options.

## Survey outcome (summary)

Nine Clanks surveyed across at least four lineages. **Zero** primary
surfaces imply unmeasured health; every Clank either shows genuinely
measured health (korean-tech-wire, oem-radar, feature-phone-clank,
smartwatch-clank) or keeps health clearly elsewhere-reachable
(chinese-tech-wire, semiconductor-intelligence, smartphone-clank,
tablet-clank, watch-clank). Three per-repo-authored artifacts independently
defend against exactly the implied-health risk ("Quiet ≠ healthy",
Fleet Law 3 health honesty, smartwatch's interpretation guard). DISTINCT
from COM-008 (shown-health dimension separation vs unshown-health
implication); not redundant. Objectively testable; every surveyed
implementation conforms as-is.

## Recommendation

RATIFY AS WRITTEN (agent recommendation — operator decides).

## Operator options

- **Option A — Ratify as written.** Flip STD-UI-COM-012 to RATIFIED v2
  with a decision-record reference in its notes (requires its own
  operator review notation). No implementation in the surveyed fleet
  changes.
- **Option B — Ratify with the SHOULD clause simplified.** Same as A but
  reword the reachability clause ("when operational judgement is part of
  the workflow") if the operator considers it vague. Wording polish only;
  no evidence pressure either way.
- **Option C — Retire as a COM-008 corollary.** Rejected by the dossier:
  COM-008 governs health that IS shown; COM-012 governs implying health
  that is NOT shown. Retirement would leave the implication risk
  unregulated.
- ~~Option D — Hold~~: not offered. The evidence is complete; no concrete
  missing source exists.

## Operator ruling — ACCEPTED, Option A (2026-08-31)

The operator ratified STD-UI-COM-012 as written. Recorded reasoning: the
multi-lineage evidence shows the fleet already treats "absence of visible
failure" and "measured health" as different concepts. COM-012 is not
redundant with COM-008 — COM-008 governs the semantics of health vs
coverage when health is shown; COM-012 governs what the primary workflow
may imply when health is not actually observed. STD-UI-COM-012 is
therefore RATIFIED at v2, text unchanged; traceability recorded in the
standard's notes.
